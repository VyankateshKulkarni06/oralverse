## 1. Market Landscape Overview

The dental AI market is primarily dominated by radiographic (X-ray / CBCT) diagnostic AI vendors targeting insurance claim adjudication and practice management. OralVerse identifies a critical whitespace: **clinical intraoral photographic intelligence and visual patient communication**, which remains underserved compared to radiographic AI.

## 3. Market Trends & Strategic Vectors

1. **Shift to Teledentistry & Remote Triage**: Rise in direct-to-consumer and mobile dental vans requiring rapid smartphone photo triage.
2. **Visual Patient Trust**: Patients cannot easily interpret black-and-white bitewings, but they instantly grasp high-definition intraoral photos with highlighted margins and tooth numbering.
3. **Regulatory Barriers**: 510(k) clearances are becoming more rigorous for autonomous AI, reinforcing OralVerse's CDS (Clinician-in-the-Loop) positioning.
